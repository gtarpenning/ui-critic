# Taste

Generated by `scripts/distill.py` from 17 decisions, newest 2026-11-01. Decisions beat these rules, and newer beats older.

1. **The user's own photo is the hero of any post-bearing surface, and text-only feeds lose.** Photo-first Home beat a text feed because "my photo goes nowhere" otherwise. They give up feed density: one post fills most of a screen. It stops applying on aggregate/ranking surfaces (Top rated, My List), where thumbnails stay small and the row is the unit. *(home-photo-first, home-not-compact, nophoto-unified)*

2. **A post's opened detail view must be the Home card, enlarged, not a re-layout.** Same order (author, photo, title, quote, actions, reactions); they gave up a bespoke "detail page" with its own header, pill row and boxed sections. Holds wherever a card has a detail view; it does not require the detail to add nothing, since extra sections (Friends, About) go below the card. *(post-e-over-d, post-e-over-b)*

3. **One score language everywhere: a score is a colored pill, the viewer-neutral aggregate is always the gold pill with a trophy, and the poster's score carries their avatar.** They give up per-screen bespoke score treatments (big banner numbers, bare text). Applies to every screen showing the global number; friend and own scores use the green/orange/red tint pill instead, never gold. *(post-one-score-language, nophoto-unified, mylist-one-score-language, top-clean-rows)*

4. **When a screen has no photo, fake the photo layout rather than design a different layout.** A tinted hero tile with the beer monogram and the poster's score pill beat a score banner, because the three scores need to read the same as on photo posts. They accept a large, mostly empty tile for consistency. Stops if the no-photo state becomes the common case. *(nophoto-unified, nophoto-band)*

5. **Progressive disclosure: counts, beer style, "you X.X" comparisons and rater totals are removed from summary surfaces and pushed one tap deeper.** "Everyone 8.7 · 8" became a bare gold pill; "Friends 8.5 · 3" became "Friends 8.5"; Top rated rows lost style and "You 9.8". They give up at-a-glance confidence signals (sample size). Exception: Top rated keeps "N ratings" because the count is the credibility of a global rank, and that's the one place it lives. *(beer-gold-pill, beer-no-counts, top-trim, top-clean-rows)*

6. **Cut redundancy even when it is accurate.** Don't restate a friend's score where the card already shows it; don't add explanatory lines ("N people rated · M are friends"). They give up self-explanation and rely on one clear place per fact. Does not apply to the primary-score pill, which repeats across screens on purpose (rule 3): repeating a *format* is good, repeating a *value in the same view* is not. *(home-tighter, top-trim, post-e-over-b)*

7. **Spacing is a tuning knob they notice: tighten gaps between a strip and the next section header, and match heights of sibling pills.** Small visual disorder gets rejected even when content is right. Applies to adjacent related blocks; it does not mean shrink the hero photo. *(home-tighter, beer-gold-pill)*

8. **A completion screen has exactly one primary action, and it ends the flow.** "Done" is the sole filled button; "Rank another" is removed and "Add details" stays as a quiet text link. They give up a growth/loop nudge because batch ranking isn't the normal path. Applies to post-action confirmation, not to the persistent "+" tab button. *(done-one-button)*

9. **Prefer a real preview over an icon or label that merely names a thing.** A small map thumbnail replaced the pin plus place-name chip; scores got avatars. They give up the text name of the venue on the photo. Holds when the preview is small and sits in the existing slot; it does not justify adding a large embedded map. *(location-tile, location-tile-ios)*

10. **Utility chrome goes to a corner icon, not a list row at the bottom.** Settings became a gear top-right on Profile. They give up discoverability by label. Applies to rarely used, non-content items; content entry points (Want to try, Top rated) stay as labeled rows. *(profile-gear)*

11. **Link related ranking surfaces directly, with the trophy as the bridge.** Top rated is reachable from My List by a gold-trophy row at the top, and the same trophy marks the global score elsewhere. They spend top-of-list space on navigation to tie "my ranking" and "everyone's ranking". Stops where a row would push the user's own list below the fold on a small screen. *(mylist-one-score-language, post-one-score-language)*

12. **Rank and discovery are combined with the social feed, not split into separate modes.** Home pairs a "Friends loved, you haven't had" photo strip with activity, and a stories-style avatar row plus a "Try next" list was rejected as compact but busier and less photographic. They give up quick scanning of many picks (8 picks, ranked rows) for a few big photo cards. Applies on Home only; list surfaces remain dense rows. *(home-not-compact, home-photo-first)*
